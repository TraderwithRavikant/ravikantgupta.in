The small print

Disclaimer and privacy

Education only

Everything on this site is for education and is my personal analysis. It is not investment advice, a research report, or a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any share, derivative or crypto asset.

No tips, no calls

I don't give tips, trading calls, price targets or signals: not here, not on social media, not in messages. If anyone offers you these in my name, it isn't me.

Past data is not a promise

Data Diary entries describe how markets behaved in the past. Lab entries test ideas on past data. Neither tells you what will happen next. Results on past data, including backtests, do not predict future results.

Trading involves risk

Trading, especially futures, options and crypto, can lose money, sometimes quickly and sometimes more than you expect. Make your own decisions, and consult a qualified, SEBI-registered professional where appropriate.

Mistakes

I check my numbers carefully and show the method behind each one, but mistakes are possible. If you find one, tell me through any of my accounts. I'll correct it and say so on the page.

Books and other people's ideas

Books on the shelf are listed for learning. When an idea comes from a book or a person, I name them. Naming a book or a person doesn't mean they endorse this site.

Privacy

  • This site sets no cookies and asks you to sign up for nothing.
  • Your light or dark choice is saved in your own browser only.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers to fetch them.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.
  • If a newsletter is added later, this section will name the service that holds your email and explain how to unsubscribe.

Links to other sites

Links to other sites are there for convenience. I'm not responsible for what those sites say or do.
