Lab #01: The trend-following pullback

Topic: Trend & setups · Source: Alexander Elder's "value zone", The New Trading for a Living · 23 Oct 2026

The idea

Trade only in the direction of the trend, and only after price has come back to a fair level instead of chasing it. Simple to say. The question is whether it survives real data and real costs.

Where it comes from

A classic trend-following pullback. Alexander Elder's "value zone" in The New Trading for a Living, buying pullbacks towards the moving averages in an uptrend, is a close relative. It's on my shelf.

The rule

IF daily higher highs + higher lows
AND price pulls back to the 20-period EMA
AND a bullish candle closes there on above-average volume
THEN enter at the next open
STOP below the recent swing low
SIZE from the stop, so a loss costs a small, fixed share of capital

Every word is a rule a computer could follow. That's the point.

How it will be tested

  • Daily bars built from my one-minute futures data, 2018 to 2026.
  • The rule is fixed before the test. It's built on the first 70% of the data, then run unchanged on the last 30%, which it has never seen.
  • Brokerage, STT, exchange charges, GST and slippage are all included.
  • Fewer than 100 trades means the result is called weak.

Result

Not tested yet. This is the next test in the lab. The result will go here with its worst drawdown right next to it, including if the idea fails.

Limits

Margin rules have changed since 2018. One market, one period. Past data flatters every idea, which is exactly why the unseen 30% matters.

Education only. This is a test of an idea, not a recommendation to trade it.
