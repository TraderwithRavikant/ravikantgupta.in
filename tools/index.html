The arithmetic behind each tool

  • Losing streak. After n losses in a row, risking r of your account each time, you have (1 − r)ⁿ of it left. Getting back to where you started needs a gain of 1 ÷ (1 − r)ⁿ − 1. That's why the bars shrink faster than they grow back.
  • Expectancy. (win rate × average win) − (loss rate × average loss). Above zero is an edge on paper, before it has been tested on real data with costs.
  • Position size. Shares = (capital × risk %) ÷ (entry − stop), rounded down. A wider stop means fewer shares for the same risk.
