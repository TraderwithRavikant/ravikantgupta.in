Logic building · four steps

From a hunch to a written rule

A rule is a decision you make once, calmly, so you don't have to make it again in the heat of the market. This is how one gets built, step by step.

Step 1 of 4

Catch the hunch

Every rule starts as a feeling: "gaps always fill", "this level always holds". Write it down word for word before you trade on it. A hunch you haven't written down can quietly change every time you look at a chart.

Step 2 of 4

Define every word

A hunch hides vague words. Replace each one with something you can measure. If two people could read the sentence and reach different answers, it isn't defined yet.

Step 3 of 4

Write it as IF / THEN

Turn the defined words into a rule that leaves nothing to judgement: when it applies, what you do, where you're wrong and how much you risk. If you can't write the stop and the size, you don't have a rule yet.

Step 4 of 4

Test before you trust

Run it on past data with charges, slippage and taxes, and look at the drawdown, not just the profit. Then check it on data it has never seen. Most hunches don't survive. The ones that do get followed without negotiating.
