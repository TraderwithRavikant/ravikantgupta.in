before any trade

  1. Is this my rule, or someone else's tip?
  2. Have I tested it, or only read about it?
  3. Do I know how much it can lose in a bad stretch?
  4. Does today's market look like the one I tested?
  5. Will today's margin let me hold it?
  6. Are charges and slippage in my numbers?

Logic building · four steps

From a hunch to a written rule

A rule is a decision you make once, calmly, so you don't have to make it again in the heat of the market. This is how one gets built, step by step.

1 · read the structure

Before any setup, one question: is price making higher highs and higher lows, lower ones, or neither? That's the trend, defined by a rule, not by a feeling.

2 · wait for the setup

In an uptrend I don't chase. I wait for price to come back towards its 20-period average. No pullback, no trade. Chasing is how good ideas become bad entries.

3 · ask the volume

Price tells you where the market went. Volume tells you whether anyone meant it. I want the turn back up to come on above-average volume.

4 · risk before reward

The stop goes below the last swing low. The size comes from the stop, so a loss costs a small, fixed share of capital. Then the rule is written down, tested on data, and followed without negotiating.

IF higher highs + higher lows
AND a pullback to the 20-period average
AND an up-close on above-average volume
THEN enter · stop below the swing low · size from the stop
