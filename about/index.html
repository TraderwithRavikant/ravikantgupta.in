About

Who's writing this

“A disciplined trader who now works hard to make other people systematic and disciplined.”

College friends got me into markets in 2018. Social media was exploding and markets were suddenly everywhere. I started reading, then trading.

I lost money. I stopped. I started again. When I came back, I came back with rules, and a habit of testing before trusting.

Today I trade intraday, swing, positional and options in Indian markets, and crypto too. I studied Electrical Engineering, and I read constantly: newspapers, magazines, books from different genres, even children's books. If I'm proud of one thing, it isn't a number. It's that I never stopped learning.

How I work

Rules I keep

So you know how to read everything on this site.

  • No tips, calls or targetsI share how I think and what the data shows. Never what to buy or sell.
  • Rules before tradesEvery trade has a written reason, a stop and a size before I enter.
  • Risk before rewardI decide what I can lose before I think about what I can make.
  • Data over opinionsIdeas get checked against data, with costs included, before I trust them.
  • Mistakes get publishedFailed tests and my own errors go up too.
  • Credit givenIf an idea came from a book or a person, I name them.

Official accounts only. I never give tips or ask for money in messages. If someone does in my name, it isn't me. See my accounts.
